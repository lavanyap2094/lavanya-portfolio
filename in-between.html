Between each movement lies a story — like a breath held, a rhythm shifting, a moment becoming. Dance, like life, is not only in the steps we take but in the spaces between them. In the quiet unraveling of movement, beauty lingers — fluid, fleeting, endlessly alive. This series is an attempt to capture the fleeting beauty of the in-between.

Creative Direction
In Between

IDEA

I started thinking about the moments that exist between movement and stillness — those few seconds when a body is shifting, a gesture is changing, or a pose hasn't quite settled yet.

I wanted to capture those moments rather than focusing on a perfect pose or a finished movement. There is something about that in-between space that feels more honest and raw.

The project became an exploration of how movement can communicate emotion without having to be completely defined.

VISUAL DIRECTION

I wanted the images to feel expressive, warm, and slightly dreamlike.

Movement became a big part of how I approached this series, allowing the fabric, the body, and the surroundings to interact rather than treating them as separate elements. I was interested in the way a flowing silhouette could change from one frame to the next, and how small gestures could completely change the feeling of an image.

I kept the visual direction fairly intuitive. Rather than making every frame feel perfectly controlled, I wanted to leave room for those unexpected moments that happen when a dancer is actually moving.

STORY

The series follows a feeling rather than a literal narrative.

It moves between moments of stillness and movement, capturing fragments rather than trying to document one continuous action. Each image feels like a moment taken from somewhere larger — like a gesture that has just happened, or one that is about to happen.

For me, the in-between is where the story lives.
