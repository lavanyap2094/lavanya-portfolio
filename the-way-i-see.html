A collection of moments from my everyday life that reflect the way I naturally see the world. I'm drawn to unexpected details, light, texture, colour, movement and composition. Photography has become a way for me to understand and refine that instinct, and it directly shapes how I approach creative and art direction: observing closely, finding connections, and seeing possibility in the ordinary.

Photography
The Way I See

I've always noticed things that I don't necessarily know how to explain.

A shadow falling across a wall. The way light changes a familiar place. A strange shape in nature. A colour combination that catches me off guard. Small details that most of the time would just pass by.

These photographs come from my everyday life, moments I stopped for because something about them felt worth keeping. There wasn't always a plan or a story behind them. Sometimes I just saw something differently and wanted to remember it.

Over time, I think this has become a way of understanding myself too. Photography has taught me to slow down and pay attention, and to trust my instinct when something feels visually interesting, even if I can't immediately explain why. There is always a sense of connection and some element which draws me in.

This collection is really about the way I look — my curiosity, my sensitivity to detail, and my tendency to find beauty in places and moments that might otherwise go unnoticed.
