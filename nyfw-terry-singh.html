THE PROJECT

Working with Terry Singh NYC for the Spring/Summer 2023, Fall/Winter 2023, Spring/Summer 2024 collections gave me the opportunity to be part of a fashion project from the development stage through to its presentation at New York Fashion Week.

I assisted with the design development and styling of the collection, working through garment construction, fittings and the details that eventually came together on the runway.

What interested me most was the balance within the collection — traditional menswear references alongside softer, more fluid silhouettes. There was a sense of structure, but it never felt rigid.

My role was very hands-on. I worked through look coordination, fittings and styling, while also helping make sure the visual direction of the collection translated from the clothes themselves into the final presentation.

THE PROCESS

For me, the most valuable part of the project was seeing how an idea changes once it enters a real production environment.

A look can work beautifully on its own and still need to be reconsidered once it is on a body, next to another look, under studio lighting, or moving down a runway.

I was involved in those small decisions - adjusting, rearranging, refining and figuring out what made the collection feel cohesive as a whole.

That process made me think about styling less as simply putting an outfit together, and more as building a visual rhythm across an entire collection.

NEW YORK FASHION WEEK

The final presentation brought all of those decisions into one space.

These images represent the looks as they came together for New York Fashion Week, focusing on the silhouettes, styling and details that shaped the collection as a whole.

I wanted the final images to reflect not just the individual pieces, but the visual language and identity of the collection.
