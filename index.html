MY STORY
I've always been drawn to seeing things from a different perspective — finding connections, emotions, and possibilities in the details around me.

This way of seeing shapes how I approach a concept, allowing me to find unexpected narratives and translate them into visual worlds that feel distinctly my own.

My journey began with styling and grew through fashion design, photography, and eventually into creative and art direction, shaping the way I approach visual storytelling.
